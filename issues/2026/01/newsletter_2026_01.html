CfP Workshop, DeZIM: Fragmented and Contested? Debates on Racism in Media

The Fragmented and Contested? Debates on Racism in Traditional and Social Media international workshop brings together scholars to examine how racism becomes visible, normalized, contested, or dismissed across platforms and political contexts. Hosted by DeZIM – German Center for Integration and Migration Research in Berlin on 12 June 2026, and organized by Nader Hotait, Tom Runge, and Elias Steinhilper, the workshop is especially relevant for political communication researchers interested in media salience, framing, algorithms, editorial practice, and societal effects on racialized communities.

The organizers invite 250–300 word abstracts plus a short bio (max 150 words) by 31 January 2026 to hotait@dezim-institut.de. This is an author workshop: selected participants will discuss full papers in detail and may be invited to submit 6,000–8,000 word manuscripts for a special issue (full papers due 31 May 2026). Submit an abstract or contact the organizers for queries — contribute empirical, comparative, theoretical, or methodological work that advances how we study racism in media.

Deadline: 31 January 2026

CfP: Special Issue on Co-Producing Environmental Publics in Communication and the Public

This is a Call for Papers for a Special Issue in Communication and the Public titled Co-Producing Environmental Publics: Technology, Communication, and Ecological Transformation. The issue examines how digital technologies—sensor networks, big data, algorithms and AI—reshape the communicative infrastructures through which environmental publics form, influence perceptions of risk, and mediate responsibility and action.

Contributions are invited on themes such as algorithmic infrastructures, datafication of environmental knowledge, networked activism, surveillance ecologies, and misinformation, with a particular encouragement for work from Asia, Africa, Latin America, and Indigenous contexts and for interdisciplinary approaches. Guest editors are Dr. Dechun Zhang (University of Copenhagen), Dr. Weiai Xu (University of Massachusetts Amherst), and Dr. Han Lin (Soochow University); submit an abstract (up to 500 words, in English) to the guest editors with the subject line “CAP Special Issue Submission.”

Deadline: Abstracts: March 20, 2026; Notification of invitations: March 30, 2026; Full papers: July 31, 2026. Planned publication: 2027. Clear call to action: prepare and submit your abstract by March 20, 2026 and consult the full call for details.

CfP: Sintaxis Special Issue on Communicability

The special issue of Revista Sintaxis on Interdisciplinary Perspectives: Contemporary Challenges and Critical Alternatives in Practical Philosophy and Communication invites original, unpublished work exploring the intersections of communicability, practical philosophy, and the humanities. Topics include: epistemologies of communication, digital ethics, post-truth and democracy, algorithmic mediation, scientific discourse, and the role of education and culture in public life.

Submissions are welcomed from scholars across the humanities and social sciences using qualitative, quantitative, mixed-methods, or theoretical approaches. Guest editors Dr. Ignacio Quepons and Dr. José Ramón Orrantia Cavazos (Instituto de Filosofía, Universidad Veracruzana) will consider contributions that include empirical research, critical dissemination, or situated theoretical reflection. Submission is via the journal’s OJS platform; author guidelines are available on the journal site.

Submission Deadline: March 30, 2026
