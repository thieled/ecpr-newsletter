## CFP: Special Issue "An Emotional Divide"

::: section

The *“An Emotional Divide: The Relationship between Media and Politics in Times of Perpetual Crisis”* special issue of The International Journal of Press/Politics invites extended abstracts for research examining how emotions shape journalism and political communication amid ongoing crises. Guest editors **Lenka Waschková Císařová (Masaryk University, Czechia)**, **Alena Kluknavská (Masaryk University, Czechia)**, **Sophie Lecheler (University of Vienna, Austria)**, and **Folker Hanusch (University of Vienna, Austria)** welcome work that maps emotional dynamics across crises, compares national and cultural patterns, and interrogates how emotions are mobilised by journalists and political actors—especially in relation to disinformation, AI, affective polarisation, and crisis reporting.

Submissions should be extended abstracts of 500 words (excluding references) and may adopt diverse methods and genres. Submit your abstract as a single PDF (including author names, affiliations, and bios) to cisarova@fss.muni.cz by **2 February 2026**; authors will be notified by **2 March 2026**, and full papers are due **30 June 2026**. Please prepare abstracts that clearly state theoretical framing, methods, and expected contribution. Call to action: send your abstract and author information to the guest editors by the deadline to be considered for this timely, interdisciplinary special issue.

Abstract deadline: **2 February 2026**
