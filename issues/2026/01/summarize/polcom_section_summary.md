## Call: Political Communication Section — ECPR General Conference 2026 (S69)

::: section

The Political Communication Section invites submissions to *Political Communication Between Disruptive Technologies and Democratic Backsliding*, a call for panels and papers exploring how emerging technologies, information disorders, media institutions, and political actors interact to shape democratic resilience and decline. This Section, chaired by **Johanna Burger** and **Thomas Milic**, is endorsed by the ECPR Standing Group on Political Communication and will convene at the **ECPR General Conference 2026** hosted by **Jagiellonian University, Kraków**, **8–11 September 2026**.

We welcome theoretical, empirical, and methodological diversity (experimental, survey, digital-trace, computational, qualitative, mixed-methods, comparative) on topics including generative AI and platform dynamics, disinformation and cross-border influence operations, the evolving role of journalism and fact-checking, and political strategies to mitigate democratic vulnerabilities. Submit panel and paper proposals by the deadline and consult the full guidelines for panel submission rules and presenter responsibilities.

Submission deadline: **9 January 2026**
