## Ad Astra Doctoral Scholarship in Authoritarian Politics

::: section

Applications are open for the *Ad Astra Doctoral Scholarship in Authoritarian Politics* at the **School of Politics and International Relations, University College Dublin**, offering doctoral supervision under **Dr. Nikita Khokhlov**. The scholarship supports projects examining authoritarian legitimation, propaganda, disinformation, media and censorship, elite behaviour, public opinion, diffusion and learning among regimes, democratization/autocratization, and personalist rule—topics central to political communication research.

Proposals using quantitative, computational, or mixed methods (e.g., natural language processing, machine learning, survey experiments, network analysis) are particularly encouraged, though innovative qualitative approaches are also welcome. The award covers tuition (up to the non‑EU rate), provides a **€25,000** annual stipend and a **€4,000** research budget for conferences, fieldwork, and related costs.

Apply by **Friday 6th February 2026, 5pm (Dublin time)**. If you are interested in advancing research on communication under authoritarianism, consider applying now.
