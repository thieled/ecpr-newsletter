## Call for Papers: Sintaxis Special Issue on Communicability

::: section

The *Interdisciplinary Perspectives: Contemporary Challenges and Critical Alternatives in Practical Philosophy and Communication* special issue of Revista Sintaxis (Facultad de Comunicación, Centro de Investigación para la Comunicación Aplicada) invites original, unpublished manuscripts that examine the reciprocal relationship between communicability, practical philosophy, and the humanities. The issue foregrounds themes such as the epistemological foundations of communicability, ethics of digital communication, truth and post-truth, democracy and deliberation, technology and algorithmic mediation, scientific communication, and the role of education and cultural narratives in contemporary public discourse.

Submissions are welcomed from scholars across the humanities and social sciences using qualitative, quantitative, mixed-methods, or theoretical approaches. Guest editors **Dr. Ignacio Quepons** and **Dr. José Ramón Orrantia Cavazos** (Instituto de Filosofía, **Universidad Veracruzana**), together with the Sintaxis editorial team at **Universidad Anáhuac México**, will consider contributions that include empirical research, critical dissemination, or situated theoretical reflection. Submission is via the journal’s OJS platform; author guidelines are available on the journal site. Please submit by **March 30, 2026**.

Deadline: **March 30, 2026**
