## Elgar Encyclopedia of Political Communication — new reference work

::: section

*Elgar Encyclopedia of Political Communication*, edited by **Alessandro Nai**, **Max Grömping**, and **Dominique Wirz**, is a newly published comprehensive reference that maps the field of political communication, including media and journalism studies. The volume comprises **431 entries** authored by **581 contributors from 58 countries**, and presents authoritative overviews of foundational theories, empirical methodologies, and emerging research directions (DOI: https://doi.org/10.4337/9781035301447).

This encyclopedia is a key resource for scholars seeking synthesis and inspiration across subfields — from agenda-setting and media effects to digital communication and comparative perspectives. The editors’ introductory chapter, “Mapping political communication,” is available open access: https://www.elgaronline.com/display/book/9781035301447/front-7.xml. Consult the volume for literature reviews, conceptual framing, and methodological guidance.
