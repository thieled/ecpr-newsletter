## A drizzle, not a storm: Polarisation around climate change in Dutch debates

::: section

Join **Edwin Jans** (**Wageningen University**) for a concise presentation of *A drizzle, not a storm: Polarisation around climate change in dutch political and media debates*, which uses a longitudinal manual content analysis (2010–2021) of parliamentary debates and newspapers to measure three forms of polarisation: ideological, affective, and factual belief. The study finds stable ideological divides, while affective and factual-belief polarisation remain low but show modest increases recently—trends linked in politics to the rise of ecological and populist radical-right parties and in media to popular newspapers.

The paper will be presented in session S7, chaired by **Mariken van der Velden** (**VU Amsterdam**), on **Tuesday 11:00–11:45 GMT (10/02/2026)**. Attend online to hear preliminary results and planned further analyses; read the panel details or join the session via the links below.
