## CFP: Co-Producing Environmental Publics (Communication and the Public)

::: section

This is a Call for Papers for a Special Issue in *Communication and the Public* titled *Co-Producing Environmental Publics: Technology, Communication, and Ecological Transformation*. The issue examines how digital technologies—sensor networks, big data, algorithms and AI—reshape the communicative infrastructures through which environmental publics form, influence perceptions of risk, and mediate responsibility and action.

Contributions are invited on themes such as algorithmic infrastructures, datafication of environmental knowledge, networked activism, surveillance ecologies, and misinformation, with a particular encouragement for work from **Asia, Africa, Latin America,** and **Indigenous contexts** and for interdisciplinary approaches. Guest editors are **Dr. Dechun Zhang (University of Copenhagen)**, **Dr. Weiai Xu (University of Massachusetts Amherst)**, and **Dr. Han Lin (Soochow University)**; submit an abstract (up to 500 words, in English) to the guest editors with the subject line “CAP Special Issue Submission.”

Deadline: **Abstracts: March 20, 2026; Notification of invitations: March 30, 2026; Full papers: July 31, 2026**. Planned publication: **2027**. Clear call to action: prepare and submit your abstract by **March 20, 2026** and consult the full call for details.
