## Postdoctoral Researcher — POLSTYLE (ERC) at University of Namur

::: section

The Research Institute Transitions at **University of Namur** invites applications for a two‑year postdoctoral position on the ERC‑funded project *POLSTYLE — New trends or old habits? Stability and changes of political styles since 1960*, coordinated by **Prof. Jeremy Dodeigne**. POLSTYLE maps long‑term changes in political communication using the Political Hostility Scale (PHOS) and unique datasets from parliamentary debates, television archives and social media, combining theory, comparative analysis and cutting‑edge NLP methods.

The appointment is ideal for a candidate with a PhD in comparative politics and strong interests in political communication, democratic erosion, party politics and/or radical right studies. The postdoc will develop an independent research agenda within the project (topics include mainstreaming of hostile rhetoric, spill‑over across arenas, effects on citizens, and party communication strategies), lead publications, and work with a team using advanced computational tools; computational support is provided. The position is based in **Namur, Belgium**, offers mentorship and conference funding, and starts **1 or 15 October 2026**. Apply by sending a single PDF (cover letter, 2‑page research report, CV, and three referees) to polstyle@unamur.be.

**Deadline: 15 August 2026**
