## Call for Papers — Beyond Fragmentation? (AssoComPol Annual Conference)

::: section

The Italian Association of Political Communication (AssoComPol) invites submissions to *Beyond Fragmentation? Contemporary Forms of Belonging Between Ideology and Narrative*, its annual conference exploring how new modes of political-cultural belonging emerge amid fragmentation, polarization, and digital mediation. The programme welcomes theoretical and empirical work on themes such as values and political culture, pre-political citizenship and affect, online communities and subcultures, journalism and alternative news ecosystems, the role of platforms and AI, and methodological approaches to cultural transformation.

The conference will take place at the Departments of Political Science (**University of Perugia**) and International Human and Social Sciences (**University for Foreigners of Perugia**) in **Perugia, Italy**, **10–12 December 2026**. The Scientific Committee includes scholars such as **Paolo Mancini** and **Giampietro Mazzoleni**. Submit extended abstracts (5000 characters) and required metadata via the conference submission site by **16 September 2026**. Notifications will be sent **10–12 October 2026**; full papers are due **30 November 2026**. 

Submit your proposal and consult abstract instructions online.

Deadline: **16 September 2026**
