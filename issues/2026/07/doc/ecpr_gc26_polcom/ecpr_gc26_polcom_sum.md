## Our Section at ECPR Krakow: Full Panel Schedule Is Out

::: section
![](https://thieled.github.io/ecpr-newsletter/issues/2025/12/img/Krakow_sq.png){.newsimg width="180"}

The programme for our section, **"Political Communication Between Disruptive Technologies and Democratic Backsliding,"** at the ECPR General Conference 2026 in Krakow (8–11 September) is now live. Across nine panels, papers address AI and platform governance, disinformation, election campaigning, authenticity and emotion, media trust, migration discourse, polarization, and political communication in war and conflict. All panels meet in person at the Faculty of International and Political Studies (Room 08).

| Date | Time (CEST) | Panel |
|---|---|---|
| Tue 08/09 | 09:00–10:45 | Platforms, Algorithms, and Information Control |
| Tue 08/09 | 11:15–13:00 | Conspiracy Narratives and Elite Communication |
| Tue 08/09 | 14:00–15:45 | War, Conflict, and Information Politics |
| Tue 08/09 | 16:15–18:00 | Migration, Intolerance, and Moral Boundaries |
| Wed 09/09 | 11:15–13:00 | Authenticity and Emotions |
| Wed 09/09 | 16:15–18:00 | Elections, Campaigning, and Political Advertising |
| Thu 10/09 | 09:00–10:45 | Disinformation, Falsehoods, and Democratic Resilience |
| Thu 10/09 | 11:15–13:00 | Political Alignment, Identity, and Polarization |
| Thu 10/09 | 16:15–18:00 | Media, Trust, and Democratic Accountability |

Room, date and time for the Standing Group Business Meeting and the [ECR Mentoring Session](https://ecpr.eu/Events/Event/PanelDetails/18012) are still to be announced.
