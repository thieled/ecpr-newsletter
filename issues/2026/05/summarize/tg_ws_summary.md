## Pre-conference: Instant Messaging as Political Communication Infrastructure

::: section

Join the pre-conference *Instant Messaging as Political Communication Infrastructure*, which brings together scholars to advance theory and methods for studying instant messaging platforms (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Viber) as sites of political communication and everyday governance. The event will take place on **7 September 2026** at the **Faculty of Social Studies, Masaryk University, Brno, Czech Republic** and is organized around two thematic tracks — **Pro-Democratic Political Communication** (grassroots organizing, movements, campaigns, ethics) and **Anti-Democratic Political Communication** (conspiracist/extremist networks, hate speech, authoritarian uses).

Submissions (1,000–1,500 words) are invited for original research, theoretical and methodological papers, toolkits, and data repositories. Submission deadline: **15 May 2026**; notifications by **12 June 2026**. Registration fees: ECREA members €40 | Non-members €60 | PhD students (ECREA members) €25. For queries contact **o.pasitselska@rug.nl**. Organizers include **Kilian Buehling**, **Olga Pasitselska**, **Qinfeng Zhu**, **Eedan Amit‑Danhi**, and **Emilija Gagrčin** (Universität Tübingen / Weizenbaum Institute; University of Groningen; University of Bergen). Submit your proposal and read the full call to contribute to methodological and conceptual advances in political communication research.

Submission deadline: **15 May 2026**
