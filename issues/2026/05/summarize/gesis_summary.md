## GESIS Fall Seminar in Computational Social Science (31 Aug–29 Sep)

::: section

Registration is open for the *GESIS Fall Seminar in Computational Social Science*, a month-long programme offered by **GESIS** from **31 August–29 September**, available in **Mannheim or online**. The seminar delivers introductory and advanced courses on collecting and analyzing digital behavioral data from the web, social media, and digital archives, combining lectures by leading experts with hands-on exercises and peer exchange.

Key courses this year include Web Data Collection, Machine Learning for Text Analysis, Computer Vision, Agent-Based Modeling & Simulation, Large Language Models, Causal Machine Learning, and Mobile Data Collection—topics directly relevant to scholars of political communication working with digital trace data and automated text/image analysis. Visit the course pages to view descriptions and register.
