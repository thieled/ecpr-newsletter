## VIRUS Kick‑off Seminar: Narrative Justification of Russian Violence

::: section

Join the *VIRUS* kick‑off seminar presenting *Violence by Russia in Ukraine: Examining the Implications of Its Narrative Justification in Russia and Europe*. The event frames ongoing research on how narratives that justify violence shape public opinion and political communication across Russia and European contexts.

The seminar is chaired by **Matteo Bassoli** and presented by **Polina Zavershinskaia**, with discussant **Pietro De Perini**. It takes place at **Aula Alpi, Department of Political Science, Law and International Studies (SPGI), via del Santo 26, Padua, Italy**, on **10 June 2026, 12:30–14:00** and will be offered in hybrid format (online access via Zoom). For questions and participation details, contact **polina.zavershinskaia@unipd.it**. Please register or reach out to confirm attendance.

When: **10 June 2026, 12:30–14:00**
