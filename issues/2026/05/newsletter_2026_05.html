Junior–Senior Mentoring in Krakow

Are you attending the ECPR General Conference 2026 in Krakow? We are organizing a Junior–Senior Mentoring initiative in political communication.

The idea is simple: we match junior and senior scholars based on shared research interests, and you meet once informally during the conference to exchange ideas, experiences, and advice. Interested in joining?

Please sign up–as senior or junior scholar–by submitting your details below and we’ll get in touch with you. Please note that we have limited availibilities for the junior scholars.

Deadline to register is May 23.

Volunteer for the Best Paper Award ’25 Jury!

We’re still short of jury members for our Best Paper Award (BPA) comittee!

If you want to volunteer to become part of the BPA committee to select the best paper presented in the PolComm section at the ECPR General Conference 2025 in Thessaloniki, please sign up and we’ll get in touch with you.

Deadline to register is May 20.
