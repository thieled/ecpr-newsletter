## Call: Sintaxis No. 18 — Government, Society and Media in Digital Convergence

::: section

Revista Sintaxis (Facultad de Comunicación, Centro de Investigación para la Comunicación Aplicada) invites submissions to the special monograph *Relationships and Conflicts between Government, Society, and Media in Scenarios of Digital Technological Convergence* (issue *Sintaxis No. 18*). This call seeks original, unpublished empirical and theoretical work addressing how digital technological convergence reshapes interactions among governments, civil society, and media—covering themes from transparency and civic engagement to regulation, censorship, misinformation, and new business models. Guest editors include **Dr. James A. Dettleff (Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú)**, **Dr. Nicolás Rotelli (Universidad Blas Pascal, Córdoba)**, and **Dra. Patricia Torreblanca (UNAM)**; publication is scheduled for **January 2027**.

Contributions from communication studies, political science, sociology, anthropology, cultural studies, gender studies, and related fields are welcome—using qualitative, quantitative, or mixed methods. Key topics include digital culture and narratives, legal reforms and platform regulation, freedom of expression, disinformation and AI, digital political violence, media concentration and polarization, and case studies of conflicts or alliances among political, social, and media actors. Submit manuscripts by **August 30, 2026** following the journal’s submission guidelines.

Deadline: **August 30, 2026**
