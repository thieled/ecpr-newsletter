
With the Political Communication division of the Netherlands – Flanders Communication Association (NeFCA) we organize a few seminars around PolCom classics which might also be interesting for an audience outside of Belgium/The Netherlands. There is still one more this semester on June 24: 

The NeFCA Political Communication division is pleased to invite you to the final seminar of this semester’s PolCom Classics series, featuring Sanne Kruikemeier from Wageningen University & Research. In this session, titled Artificial Intelligence, she will discuss the role of AI in contemporary political communication and its implications for media and society. The seminar will take place online via Microsoft Teams on June 24th from 12:00 to 13:00. The talk will be of interest to both early career researchers and more senior scholars working on political communication, media, and digital technologies. Participation is free of charge, but registration is required. You can register through the following link: https://forms.gle/Rotfwoi52xreXsap9.
