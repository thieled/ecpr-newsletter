## ECR Club: Presenters' Sign‑Up (Political Communication)

::: section

The *ECR Club on Political Communication*, organised by the **Standing Group on Political Communication of the ECPR**, is opening sign‑up for presenters. Early‑career researchers are invited to submit a short abstract and basic details to receive structured peer feedback on ongoing work.

Sessions are held around lunchtime on a **Tuesday**; presenters will be asked for name, contact, seniority (PhD year / year of defence), an informative title and brief abstract, and may suggest discussants. The sign‑up survey collects personal data only to organise sessions (see the ECPR privacy policy: https://ecpr.eu/Privacy).

Interested? Complete the presenter sign‑up form to propose a slot and indicate your month preference.
