## ECR Club: Sign‑Up for the Next Round!

::: section

![Screenshot of Doris Bukman's (UvA) ECR session on "Publishers' power: The influence of ownership on the framing of media mergers", discussant: Sophie Lecheler (U Vienna)](https://thieled.github.io/ecpr-newsletter/issues/2026/06/img/ecr_screen.jpg){.newsimg width="360"}

Our **Early Carreer Researcher (ECR) Club** is going in its next round! The club is a recurring series offering early-career researchers a chance to present ongoing work and receive structured feedback from a dedicated discussant. Sessions are held online around lunchtime on a **Tuesday**, and the format is explicitly designed to be supportive and developmental rather than high-stakes.

To sign up as a presenter, you will need to provide your name and contact details, your seniority level (PhD year or year of defence for postdocs), a working title and short abstract, and your preferred month to present. You may also suggest potential discussants. Sign-up is via the form below.
