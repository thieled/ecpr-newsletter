## ECR Club: Presenters' Sign‑Up (Political Communication)

::: section

The **ECPR Standing Group on Political Communication** is opening the next round of its *ECR Club* — a recurring series offering early-career researchers a chance to present ongoing work and receive structured feedback from a dedicated discussant. Sessions are held online around lunchtime on a **Tuesday**, and the format is explicitly designed to be supportive and developmental rather than high-stakes.

To sign up as a presenter, you will need to provide your name and contact details, your seniority level (PhD year or year of defence for postdocs), a working title and short abstract, and your preferred month to present. You may also suggest potential discussants. Sign-up is via the form below.
