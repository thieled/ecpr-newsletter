Hi and welcome to the ECR Club on Political Communication!
This Club is organised by the Standing Group on Political Communication of the European Consortium of Political Research (ECPR). All general information on the Club can be found on this page.

Please not that for this signup, we will collect personal information from you to organize the individual sessions. Your personal data will not be used for any other purpose. More information on the ECPR's privacy policy can be found here: https://ecpr.eu/Privacy

This survey serves to facilitate signing up to the Club as a Presenter. You will need to
Indicate your name and contact details and your seniority level (i.e., year of your PhD for PhD students/Year of defense for PostDocs).
We would also like you to indicate an informative title and a brief description/abstract of your research which we can use to approach potential discussants.
You can also suggest discussants if you like.
Finally, we will also ask you to indicate your preference regarding the month in which you would like to present. Please note that sessions will be organised around lunchtime on a Tuesday. Should this be difficult for you, feel free to add this in the final comment section of the survey.
If this all sounds good to you, let's get started! We are looking forward to discussing your research with you! :)
