## Postdoc — Media, Inequality, and Democratic Support (EDGE)

::: section

The *Postdoc Position "Media, Inequality, and Democratic Support"* invites applications to join the Horizon Europe EDGE project at the Amsterdam School of Communication Research. The role explores how media and digital information environments shape perceptions of inequality, blame, fairness, and democratic support—key issues for political communication research and comparative democratic resilience.

You will join an international consortium coordinated by **GESIS** (in **Cologne**) and work with **Kathleen Beckers** and **Katjana Gattermann** within the **Political Communication & Journalism** group at the **University of Amsterdam (ASCoR)**. The position is fully funded for 30 months (possible extension up to 6 months for teaching), starts on **1 October 2026**, and offers a salary in the range **€ 4.412 - € 6.433 gross per month**. Ideal candidates have a background in communication science, journalism, political science or related fields and strong quantitative skills.

Apply by the deadline and direct questions to **Kathleen Beckers (k.a.e.beckers@uva.nl)** or **Katjana Gattermann (k.gattermann@uva.nl)** — full details and application instructions are available at the link below.

Deadline: **7 June 2026**
