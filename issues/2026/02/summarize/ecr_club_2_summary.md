## Interpersonal Communication and First-Year Student Integration (Seminar)

::: section

Join a virtual seminar presenting *The role of interpersonal communication in the induction and adaptation process of first-year students to higher education*, a mixed-methods study that examines how relationships between students, teachers and administrative staff shape retention, dropout, academic satisfaction and performance. The talk highlights practical factors, barriers and strategies to support first‑year integration—topics relevant for scholars of institutional and political communication.

Presented by **Catalin Tutuianu (SNSPA, Romania)**; discussant **Mariëtte Huizinga (Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam)**; chair **Daniel Thiele (Freie Universität Berlin)**. Online on **10 March 2026, 11:00–12:00 GMT**. Register or join via the event page below.
