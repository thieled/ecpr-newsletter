## Democratic Expeditions Workshop: AI for Democratic Politics

::: section

The Central European University (CEU) Democracy Institute and the Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung invite submissions to *AI for Democratic Politics. Improving Campaigns, Strengthening Public Information, Supporting Citizen Participation*, a two-day workshop addressing the democratic risks and opportunities of AI across information, competition, and participation. Convenors **Zsofia Bocskay (CEU Democracy Institute)** and **Simge Andı (University of Exeter)** will host the event on **November 19–20, 2026** at **Central European University (CEU), Vienna, Austria**; the workshop seeks comparative, methodological, and policy-oriented work that speaks to journalists, policymakers, and civil-society actors.

Proposals should include a tentative title, a short abstract (max 250 words), and a brief author note (max 50 words). Some travel/accommodation support is available; selected participants will circulate full drafts in advance and contribute to a focused program that may lead to a special issue or edited volume. Submit proposals by **March 31, 2026, 11:59 PM CET** and direct enquiries to organizers as listed on the call page. Apply or read the full call via the link below — submissions via the form provided.

Deadline: **March 31, 2026, 11:59 PM CET**
