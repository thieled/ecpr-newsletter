## COSMOS PhD School on AI and Democracy (May 2026)

::: section

The *COSMOS PhD School* is a week-long intensive for PhD students and early‑career researchers exploring the political, social and ethical implications of generative artificial intelligence. Organized by the Center on Social Movement Studies (COSMOS) at the **Scuola Normale Superiore**, the School runs **18–22 May 2026** at **Palazzone di Cortona (AR)**, offers **32** hours of instruction in **English**, and admits up to **25** participants.

Through morning lectures and afternoon methodological labs, the programme addresses core questions—Does AI strengthen or weaken democracy?—and themes including AI ethics and democratic control, automation of labour and warfare, environmental impacts, and the future of journalism. Featured speakers include **Peter Asaro**, **Antonio Casilli**, **Mark Coeckelbergh**, **Matteo Pasquinelli**, and organisers **Marco Deseriis** and **Hans‑Joerg Trenz**. The School is tuition‑free; travel and accommodation are the participant’s responsibility.

Apply by sending a CV and a one‑page cover letter (single PDF) explaining fit with your research to **marco.deseriis@sns.it** and **hansjorg.trenz@sns.it** by **15 March 2026**. Accepted candidates will be notified by **1 April 2026**. 

Application deadline: **15 March 2026**
