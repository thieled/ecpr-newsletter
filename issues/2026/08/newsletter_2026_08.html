PolCom Section at ECPR Krakow

The programme for our section, “Political Communication Between Disruptive Technologies and Democratic Backsliding,” at the ECPR General Conference 2026 in Krakow (8–11 September) is now live. Across nine panels, papers address AI and platform governance, disinformation, election campaigning, authenticity and emotion, media trust, migration discourse, polarization, and political communication in war and conflict. All panels meet in person at the Faculty of International and Political Studies (Ground Floor, Room 08).

Vacany: Postdoctoral Researcher at U Namur

The Research Institute Transitions at University of Namur invites applications for a two‑year postdoctoral position on the ERC‑funded project POLSTYLE — New trends or old habits? Stability and changes of political styles since 1960, coordinated by Prof. Jeremy Dodeigne. POLSTYLE maps long‑term changes in political communication, combining comparative analysis with cutting‑edge NLP methods.

Ideal candidates hold a PhD in comparative politics with strong interests in political communication, democratic erosion, or party politics, and will develop an independent research agenda within the project. The position is based in Namur, Belgium, and starts 1 or 15 October 2026. Apply by sending a single PDF (cover letter, research report, CV, and three referees) to polstyle@unamur.be.

Deadline: 15 August 2026

CfP: Beyond Fragmentation? (AssoComPol Annual Conference)

The Italian Association of Political Communication (AssoComPol) invites submissions to Beyond Fragmentation? Contemporary Forms of Belonging Between Ideology and Narrative, its annual conference exploring new modes of political-cultural belonging amid fragmentation, polarization, and digital mediation. Topics include political culture, online communities, journalism, platforms and AI, and methods for studying cultural transformation.

The conference takes place 10–12 December 2026 in Perugia, Italy, hosted by the University of Perugia and the University for Foreigners of Perugia. Submit extended abstracts (5000 characters) by 16 September 2026; notifications follow 10–12 October, with full papers due 30 November 2026.

Deadline: 16 September 2026
