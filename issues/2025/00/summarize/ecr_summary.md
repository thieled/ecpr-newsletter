## PolComm ECR Club — 9 December: *An Emotional Reaction* (Roy Aulie J.)

::: section

The PolComm ECR Club invites early-career researchers to an online session presenting *An Emotional Reaction: Analysing the Public’s Response to Communication Tactics Used by Political Leaders in Norway during the COVID-19 Pandemic* by **Roy Aulie J.** This monthly forum, organised by **Olga Eisele** and **Alena Kluknavska**, is open to PhD students and postdocs (≤4 years post-PhD) affiliated with ECPR or ECREA political communication sections.

The study links framing and platformization literatures to an engagement-focused approach: a content analysis of **5,645 Facebook posts (Mar 2020–Jan 2022)** shows how different communication strategies elicit distinct emotions (e.g., anger, love) that shape comments and shares, with clear implications for political participation and democratic discourse. The paper will be discussed by **Pablo Jost** and chaired by **Nicoleta Corbu**. Join to hear feedback, ask questions, and connect with fellow ECRs.

Date: **9 December at 12:00**
