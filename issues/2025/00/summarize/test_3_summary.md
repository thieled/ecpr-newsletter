## PolComm ECR Club — *An Emotional Reaction* (9 December, online)

::: section

The PolComm ECR Club, a new monthly online forum for early career researchers organized by **Olga Eisele** and **Alena Kluknavska**, presents a session on *An Emotional Reaction: Analysing the Public’s Response to Communication Tactics Used by Political Leaders in Norway During the COVID-19 Pandemic* by **Roy Aulie J.** The paper will be discussed by **Pablo Jost** and chaired by **Nicoleta Corbu**. This forum is open to PhD students and postdocs (≤4 yrs post-PhD) affiliated with **ECPR** or **ECREA** political communication sections.

The study examines how Facebook communication strategies (e.g., criticism, crisis framing, excellence/renewal) elicit emotions like “anger” and “love,” shaping comments, shares, and broader civic engagement; it draws on a content analysis of 5,645 pandemic-era posts (March 2020–January 2022) and a multilevel empirical approach. Join to hear concise presentation, receive discussant feedback, and take part in the Q&A — register via your **MyECPR** account.

Date: **9 December, 12:00 (online)**
