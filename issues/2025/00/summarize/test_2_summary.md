## PolComm ECR Club — Session on *An Emotional Reaction: Analysing the Public’s Response to Communication Tactics Used by Political Leaders in Norway during the COVID-19 Pandemic*

::: section

The PolComm ECR Club presents an online session featuring *An Emotional Reaction: Analysing the Public’s Response to Communication Tactics Used by Political Leaders in Norway during the COVID-19 Pandemic* by **Roy Aulie J.** — part of a new monthly forum for Early Career Researchers organized by **Olga Eisele** and **Alena Kluknavska** within the ECPR/ECREA political communication communities. The paper (content analysis of **5,645 Facebook posts**, March 2020–January 2022) examines how communication strategies (e.g., criticism; crisis framing; excellence/renewal) map onto emotional reactions like “anger” and “love,” and how these emotions shape visibility, comments, shares and democratic engagement; the study will be discussed by **Pablo Jost** and chaired by **Nicoleta Corbu**. Join to hear concise presentation and feedback, and to discuss implications for framing, platformization, and engagement research.

Event: **9 December**, **12:00** (online)
