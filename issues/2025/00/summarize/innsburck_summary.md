## ECPR Joint Sessions 2026 — Workshop on Hostile Elite Communication

::: section

The ECPR Joint Sessions 2026 will host the workshop *The Rise of Hostile Communication Styles and their Consequences for the Functioning of Modern Democracies*, directed by **Jeremy Dodeigne (University of Namur)** and co-directed by **Chiara Vargiu (University of Amsterdam)**. The workshop examines the increasing use of adversarial, uncivil, intolerant, and violent elite rhetoric, its measurement beyond the broad label of “polarisation,” and its implications for democratic stability and political behaviour.

The organisers welcome conceptual, methodological and empirical contributions—especially longitudinal and comparative work—that address how hostile rhetoric evolves, how political and institutional contexts shape it, and what its democratic consequences are (for turnout, trust, cooperation, stability and policy). The workshop will take place during the Joint Sessions at **University of Innsbruck, Austria**, **7–10 April 2026**. Submit your paper by **3 December 2025**.

**Deadline: 3 December 2025**
