1) Concise summary:
The PolComm Early-Career Researcher (ECR) Club announces a monthly online forum for PhD students and postdocs in political communication (ECPR/ECREA sections). The next session, on December 9 at 12:00, will feature Roy Aulie J.’s study “An Emotional Reaction: Analyzing the Public’s Response to Communication Tactics Used by Political Leaders in Norway During the COVID-19 Pandemic,” discussed by Pablo Jost and chaired by Nicoleta Corbu. Based on a content analysis of 5,645 COVID-19–related Facebook posts (Mar 2020–Jan 2022) across nine strategies, the paper finds that criticism tends to evoke anger and increase engagement, while excellence/renewal posts generate more “love” reactions and broad sharing but fewer comments. The work situates its findings within framing, platformization, and engagement-focused theories and highlights the democratic implications of emotional engagement on social media. Registration requires logging in via MyECPR.
