## Next session PolComm ERC Club: An Emotional Reaction — Analyzing the Public’s Response to COVID-19 Communication by Political Leaders in Norway

::: section

We are pleased to introduce the PolComm ECR Club — a new monthly online forum for Early Career Researchers in political communication. Organized by Olga Eisele and Alena Kluknavska, this initiative is open to PhD students and postdocs (≤4 years post-PhD) working in the ECPR or European Communication Research and Education Association (ECREA) political communication sections. Each session features a concise 12–15 minute presentation, followed by feedback from a designated discussant and an open Q&A, offering a supportive space for early‑career scholars to share work and receive thoughtful commentary.

The next session takes place on 9 December at 12:00 and will host Roy Aulie J.’s paper titled “An Emotional Reaction: Analyzing the Public’s Response to Communication Tactics Used by Political Leaders in Norway During the COVID-19 Pandemic.” The paper will be discussed by Pablo Jost, with the session chaired by Nicoleta Corbu. The discussion will explore how Facebook-based communication strategies drive emotional responses and shape public engagement, examining how emotions like love, anger, and sadness influence visibility, participation, and the democratic implications of online political discourse. Roy Aulie J.’s empirical work analyzes 5,645 Facebook posts from March 2020 to January 2022, coding nine communication strategies (including criticism, crisis framing, and excellence/renewal) through a multilevel analysis. Notably, criticism tends to evoke anger and more comments and shares, while excellence/renewal posts generate more “love” reactions and are widely shared but provoke fewer comments.
