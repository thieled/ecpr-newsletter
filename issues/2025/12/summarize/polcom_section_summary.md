## Political Communication Between Disruptive Technologies and Democratic Backsliding

::: section

The ECPR General Conference 2026 section *Political Communication Between Disruptive Technologies and Democratic Backsliding* invites papers exploring how technological innovation and information disorders intersect with threats to democratic institutions. This section (S69), endorsed by the ECPR Standing Group on Political Communication and chaired by **Johanna Burger** and **Thomas Milic**, will convene at **ECPR General Conference 2026**, **Jagiellonian University**, **8–11 September 2026**.

Submissions should address themes such as generative AI, algorithmic curation, synthetic content and deepfakes, disinformation and influence operations, platform governance, media and fact-checking roles, and strategies for democratic resilience. The section welcomes theoretical, empirical, and methodological diversity (experimental, survey, computational, qualitative, mixed-methods, comparative). Please consult the section page and submit your abstract via the conference link.
